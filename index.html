About

An applied microeconomist studying how immigration and place-based policy shape firms, labor markets, and regions in Korea.

I am an Associate Research Fellow at the Migration Research & Training Centre (MRTC) in Gyeonggi-do, Korea. My research uses firm-level microdata and geospatial data to examine how foreign workers affect firm productivity, investment, and employment, and how regional policies and industrial agglomeration shape firm performance and regional development. My policy work for MRTC and the Korea Immigration Service includes estimating appropriate work-visa issuance levels and analyzing the economic effects of foreign labor.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Seoul in 2024.
